 What is Zyngram? 
 Zyngram's physical, real-world franchise structure has five levels, from smallest to largest: Point, Center, Hub, Command, and Headquarters (HQ). Separate from the physical franchise hierarchy, Zyngram also tracks a digital hierarchy for mapping and analytics purposes: Node, Zone, Territory, Region, and Nation, from smallest to largest. Zyngram is a hyperlocal franchise-based service platform. It connects customers to everyday services such as mobile recharge, utility bill payments, and local deliveries through a franchise network spread across India.

Which level covers a whole state? 
 A Point is the smallest unit, typically covering a single neighborhood. Several Points report to one Center, which usually covers a town or a city area. Several Centers report to one Hub, which covers a district-level area. Several Hubs report to one Command, which covers a state-level area. All Commands report to Zyngram HQ, which sets policy for the entire network.

 How do I get my money back if a bill payment fails? 
 Utility Bill Payment lets customers pay electricity, water, and gas bills through the Zyngram app. The customer enters their consumer number, the current bill amount is fetched, and payment is confirmed the same way as a recharge order, through a validate-then-confirm flow. If a recharge order fails after the amount is deducted, Zyngram automatically reverses the payment to your original payment method within 2 to 5 business days. If a Mobile Recharge or Bill Payment order fails after payment has been deducted, the amount is automatically reversed to the customer's original payment method within 2 to 5 business days. Customers can track a refund's status from the Order Status screen in the app.
